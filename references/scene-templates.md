# Scene Templates

Use this reference to choose structure by output type.

## 1. Article Cover Or Social Header

Goal: one clear idea at a glance.

Use:

- large title
- one focal visual
- strong crop
- simple byline or metadata
- limited palette

Avoid:

- explaining the entire article
- too many icons
- generic abstract gradients

## 2. Concept Illustration

Goal: make an abstract idea visible.

Use:

- one metaphor
- clear foreground/background
- restrained labels
- meaningful texture or geometry

Avoid:

- unrelated stock imagery
- fake human illustrations
- too many metaphors at once

## 3. Infographic Or Data Visualization

Goal: make a pattern, comparison, or relationship clear.

Use:

- accurate data
- source notes
- chart type chosen by question
- strong hierarchy
- readable labels
- consistent units

Avoid:

- fake stats
- decorative chart junk
- color without semantic meaning

## 4. PPT Or Keynote Deck

Goal: support a live speaker.

Use:

- one idea per slide
- large readable type
- visual rhythm across slides
- section dividers
- speaker notes when useful

Avoid:

- paragraph-heavy slides
- tiny captions
- same layout repeated for every slide

## 5. PDF Report Or White Paper

Goal: sustained reading and reference.

Use:

- page grid
- headers/footers
- page numbers
- citations
- tables and figures
- consistent styles

Avoid:

- presentation-scale type
- marketing hero layouts on every page

## 6. Landing Page Or Product Website

Goal: explain offer and guide conversion.

Use:

- real product or brand signal in first viewport
- clear offer
- proof
- workflow or feature sections
- focused CTA

Avoid:

- empty marketing phrases
- abstract hero with no product
- giant cards that do not help the user decide

## 7. App UI Or Prototype

Goal: make the product behavior tangible.

Use:

- platform frame when helpful
- real content
- clear navigation
- states and feedback
- overview or flow shape

Avoid:

- static screenshots pretending to be interactive
- decorative photos
- missing edge states

## 8. Short Social Visual

Goal: fast recognition and sharing.

Use:

- high contrast
- one message
- safe margins
- strong thumbnail readability
- format-specific dimensions

Avoid:

- small text
- dense explanations
- low contrast

## Combination Examples

### Product Launch Pack

- hero animation
- product landing page section
- social header
- short deck

### Research Summary

- PDF report
- executive deck
- infographic
- social teaser

### App Concept

- overview prototype
- one clickable flow
- visual direction board
- critique report

## Selection Rule

Choose the template by the user's destination, not by what is easiest to build.
